The incident-report category as configuration (`categories/incident-reports/`). Reasoning:
xref:../concepts/category-incident-reports.adoc[Incident Reports]. Repository: `isms-incidents-<year>` of the year of
awareness.

[#AUN-IR-1]
== AUN-IR-1: Report Layout and Generated Output

* An incident report must be stored in the library's layout of a per-document kind
  (https://github.com/cuioss/git-document-repository/blob/main/doc/specification/git-forms.adoc#GDR-FORMS-3[GDR-FORMS-3]): `incidents/<ulid>/document.yaml`,
  `incidents/<ulid>/narrative/*.adoc` and a generated `incidents/<ulid>/report.adoc`.
* `report.adoc` must be generated reproducibly from the YAML and the fragments; a CI check must fail when the
  committed `report.adoc` differs from a fresh generation.

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Path |Content

|`incidents/<ulid>/document.yaml`
|`title`, `occurred-at`, `detected-at`, `aware-at`, `resolved-at`, `category`, `severity`, `personal-data-affected`,
`data-subjects` (categories and approximate numbers of data subjects and records, no person), `nis2-significant`,
`affected-services`, `protection-needs`, `notifications`, `measures` (with issue ids), `timeline`, `tickets` (ids),
`root-causes`

|`incidents/<ulid>/narrative/*.adoc`
|`course.adoc`, `root-cause-analysis.adoc`, `lessons.adoc`

|`incidents/<ulid>/report.adoc`
|Generated; never edited
|===

The `<ulid>` of an incident names its directory, the working branch `refs/heads/incidents/<ulid>`, the release tag
and the link target; a readable label is the `title`. Times: `occurred-at` when the incident began, if known
(optional); `detected-at` when it was detected; `aware-at` when
the organisation became aware of it, the start of the GDPR and NIS2 deadlines (GDPR Art. 33(1), NIS2 Art. 23(4);
sources in <<AUN-IR-2>>).

[#AUN-IR-2]
== AUN-IR-2: Notification Deadlines

* When personal data are affected, the application must compute a GDPR Art. 33 notification due time of awareness
  plus 72 hours (link:https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj[Regulation (EU) 2016/679], Art. 33(1)).
* When the report is marked as a significant incident under NIS2 (`nis2-significant`), the application must compute
  an early warning (awareness +24 hours), a notification (awareness +72 hours), every intermediate report an authority
  requested, and the final report one month after the notification was sent. For an incident not resolved by then, a
  progress report meets that deadline instead, and the final report is due one month after resolution.
* The deadline rules must be data of the category's workflow, not code; due times must be shown in the form and in
  the *Due* lens, and a missed due time must stay visible in the released report.

Each row is one entry of `deadlines` in `workflow.yaml`. The rules go beyond the three deadline forms of
https://github.com/cuioss/git-document-repository/blob/main/doc/specification/git-forms.adoc#GDR-FORMS-6[GDR-FORMS-6]; extending them is an open question of
https://github.com/cuioss/git-document-repository/blob/main/doc/concepts/schema-driven-documents.adoc#_open_questions[Schema-Driven Documents, Open Questions]. `when` names a field that must be `true` or present, or
compares two times; the due time is a date field plus a period, and a deadline whose date field is still empty is
`OPEN`; the deadline is `MET` when one of its `met-by` fields holds `sent-at` ≤ due, `MISSED` after due otherwise,
`OPEN` before; a `met-by` entry with `while-unresolved` counts only when `resolved-at` is absent or later than the due
time. A field pointer with `*` yields one deadline per array element.

|`gdpr-art33`

|`aware-at` + `PT72H`

|`nis2-early-warning`
|`nis2-significant`
|`aware-at` + `PT24H`
|`notifications.nis2-early-warning`

|`nis2-notification`
|`nis2-significant`
|`aware-at` + `PT72H`
|`notifications.nis2-notification`

|`nis2-intermediate-report`

|`nis2-final-report`

The shipped periods are those of https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj[Directive (EU) 2022/2555],
Art. 23(4); how they read and where a transposition or a trust service provider differs is explained in
xref:../concepts/category-incident-reports.adoc#_the_deadline_clock[Incident Reports, The Deadline Clock]. Other
periods are a change of the category's `workflow.yaml`, not of code.

[#AUN-IR-3]
== AUN-IR-3: Mandatory Fields

* Saving must be possible with mandatory fields missing; the transitions into review and into release must be
  rejected while any mandatory field of the schema is missing, with a list of the missing fields.

States: `draft` → `in-review` → `released`; `incident.release` requires step-up. Saves validate everything but
`required`; the transitions to `in-review` and `released` validate `required` as well
(https://github.com/cuioss/git-document-repository/blob/main/doc/specification/git-forms.adoc#GDR-FORMS-2[GDR-FORMS-2]); a failure returns `MANDATORY_FIELDS_MISSING` with
the JSON Pointers of the missing fields.

[#AUN-IR-4]
== AUN-IR-4: Affected Services

* Affected services must be picked from the service catalog.
* The application must derive the protection needs of the incident per dimension as the maximum over the effective
  protection needs of the affected services (xref:directory.adoc#AUN-DIR-5[AUN-DIR-5]).

Decided provisionally with the incident reports (UC-IR-1, UC-IR-2, UC-IR-3), recorded with the bodies in
xref:rest-api.adoc#_decided_incidents[REST API Register, Open Questions]; the owner may change each one:

. *Layout* (AUN-IR-1): the fields are not at the root of `document.yaml` but in one part per stage (`triage.yaml`,
  `privacy-assessment.yaml`, `response.yaml`, `notifications.yaml`, `review.yaml`), as
  xref:../concepts/category-incident-reports.adoc#_stages_of_an_incident[Incident Reports, Stages of an Incident] says; the
  deadline rules of AUN-IR-2 read the paths of the parts (`/triage/aware-at`, `/response/resolved-at`,
  `/notifications/nis2-notification/sent-at`). The schema is in xref:rest-api.adoc#_incident_data[REST API Register, The Data
  Shape of an Incident]. The text above is not changed until the owner accepts it.
. *Deadlines* (AUN-IR-2): the status of a deadline is computed at the time of the read, at the time of the release for a
  released incident (so that `report.adoc` is reproducible), and a notification sent after the due time leaves the deadline
  `MISSED` (xref:rest-api.adoc#_incident_deadlines[REST API Register, The Deadlines]).
. *Mandatory fields* (AUN-IR-3): the transitions into review and into release are refused with `STAGES_OPEN`, the list of
  the applicable stages that are not completed with their missing fields, because a stage is completed only with its
  mandatory fields; `MANDATORY_FIELDS_MISSING` stays for a document without stages.
. *Protection needs* (AUN-IR-4): derived at the read and recorded in the generated report, not stored in a part.
